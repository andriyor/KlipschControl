# Privacy Policy

Remote for Klipsch (KRemote) does not collect, store or share any personal data.

* The app talks only to your Klipsch speaker, over Bluetooth, to read and change its volume, input and EQ settings.
* It makes no internet connections, has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and contains no third-party SDKs.
* Bluetooth permission is used only to find and control your speaker. Nothing about the speaker or your device leaves your phone.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/andriyor/KlipschControl/issues.
